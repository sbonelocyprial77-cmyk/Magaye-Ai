Learn the tools

RSI
A momentum measure from 0 to 100. Common reference levels such as 70 and 30 are not automatic buy or sell instructions.

MACD
Compares moving averages to help study momentum and possible changes in direction.

Moving average
Smooths historical prices to make a broader direction easier to inspect.

Volatility
Describes the size and speed of price changes. Volatility can increase around important news.
